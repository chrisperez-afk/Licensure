Sync Roster from NREMT

NREMT doesn't have a public agency lookup, but your NREMT organization account can export a roster of everyone certified under your agency as a spreadsheet. Export that file and upload it here to sync everyone's name, certification level, registry number, and recert cycle dates in one shot — matched by NREMT's own registry number and EMS ID so re-uploading is always safe (it updates existing records, it never duplicates them), and every certification found this way is automatically marked verified today.

People also picked up by the DSHS roster sync are matched to the same provider record where possible, so you don't end up with two entries for one person.
