Sync Roster from DSHS

Texas DSHS's provider page lists every certified person currently affiliated with your EMS Provider License under "Related Party Name." Pull that page up, select and copy the roster section (or the whole page — extra text is fine, it's ignored), and paste it below. This updates everyone in one shot: matching people by their DSHS certificate number, adding anyone new, and marking every certification found here as verified today since it just came straight from the source.

Safe to re-run any time you want a refresh — re-pasting the same or an updated roster updates existing records instead of duplicating them.

Fetch Directly (no copy/paste)

If an agency has a DSHS roster URL saved in Settings, the app can pull that page itself instead of you copying and pasting it. This also runs automatically every night if a nightly sync is configured (see the README).
